5. COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING & SOLUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ERROR: CANNOT SEND EMAIL / SMTP REJECTED]
Fix: Outgoing SMTP authentication is disabled in your app. Enable "Authentication Required"
and enter username "contact@yamato-ac.jp" and your password under Outgoing Server.

[ERROR: CERTIFICATE WARNING OR UNTRUSTED DOMAIN]
Fix: Do not use arbitrary server names. Use "mail.reiwasakura.tech".
The Let's Encrypt SSL certificate is issued and auto-renewed for mail.reiwasakura.tech,
ensuring 100% trusted verification on all iOS and Android versions.

[IMAP VS POP3: WHICH SHOULD I USE?]
- Use IMAP: If you want all emails, folders, sent messages, and read/unread status
  to sync in real-time across your iPhone, Android, Mac/PC, and Webmail.
- Use POP3: Only if you want to download emails to a single local device.
